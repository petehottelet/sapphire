# Sapphire material and prompt recipes

## Use the examples

The six bundled icon examples demonstrate optical material, compact highlights, restrained spectral fringes, and contrast. They do not require every output to be an icon, to contain their symbols, or to use a rounded-square tile. Establish the silhouette first using [silhouette workflow](silhouette-workflow.md), then apply the material while preserving the graphic's composition and geometry.

For icons, inspect the full lens and white symbol together. For typography, logos, and frameless objects, transfer the dark glass and edge effects onto the actual shapes. Retain the third-party notice with redistributed bundled example artwork.

## General graphic

"Create [GRAPHIC TYPE] depicting [EXACT SUBJECT/CONTENT] for [PURPOSE], at [ASPECT RATIO/DIMENSIONS]. Use subject reference A for content, geometry, and composition; use style reference B only for Sapphire's dark convex glass, compact blue/cyan/white caustics, restrained warm prismatic fringes, and narrow chromatic aberration. Keep most surfaces dark and visually quiet. Let refraction meet the actual silhouette without an inset ring. Preserve [IDENTIFYING FEATURES]. Background: [TRANSPARENT OR REQUESTED SETTING]. Add no unrequested text, icon tile, frame, or scene elements."

## Icon tile

"Create one front-facing rounded-square near-black optical lens containing [SUBJECT] as a crisp luminous white pictogram. Use gently convex glass, compact blue/cyan/white caustics at the upper-right and lower-left, fine warm prismatic tips, and local spectral displacement at glyph edges. Keep a broad quiet dark center. Match the reference's small highlight footprint and edge-to-edge optical finish. Transparent outside the lens. No label, inset black ring, pedestal, or isometric tilt."

## Logo or symbol

"Apply Sapphire material to the exact supplied logo silhouette. Preserve its aspect ratio, orientation, internal negative spaces, separated pieces, and relative placement. Use dark optical glass with selective compact caustics and fine cool/warm spectral fringes. Keep the background and intended holes transparent. No enclosing icon tile or substituted symbol."

## Typography

Follow the complete workflow in [typography](typography.md): render the actual font first, use that render as geometry authority, apply the material to the letterforms, and verify spelling and shape fidelity. Do not rely on a font name alone.

## Illustration or composition

"Restyle the specified forms in this illustration with Sapphire's optical material while preserving the scene, arrangement, and visual hierarchy. Keep [TEXT/ANNOTATIONS/DATA] unchanged where required. Use consistent lighting across forms, restrained local refraction, and transparent negative space where requested. Do not reduce the composition to one pictogram or introduce an icon tile."

For precise diagrams and data graphics, keep labels and measured geometry authoritative. Use exact layout/rendering tools for structure and data; limit generated artwork to decorative material treatments that do not change meaning.

## Targeted correction

"Revise only [DEFECT]. Preserve the approved geometry, wording, layout, material-support shape, and compact highlight footprints. Maintain legibility, transparent holes, and the original aspect ratio. Do not enlarge highlights or brighten the full surface as compensation."

## Interpret multimodal inputs

Match the interpretation to the requested output. A photograph may supply a subject for an icon, an object silhouette, or a complete illustration. A document may supply headline wording, a composition, or a concept. Inspect relevant source content; do not automatically miniaturize a screenshot or replace a requested layout with a symbol.
